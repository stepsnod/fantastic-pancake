How this works

The book is broken into chapters below. Open one chapter at a time and work through it. Nothing else is asked of you.

Inside a chapter are the passages a second voice might read — quotations, poems, song lyrics, dreams and visions. For each one you do six small things:

  1. Read the passage. The boxed text is exactly as it appears in your book. It is locked and cannot be changed.
  2. Choose who reads it — Catherine, you, or leave it undecided.
  3. Approve it, change the wording, leave it out, or mark it to discuss.
  4. Set the rights status.
  5. Add a note if there is anything you want to tell me.

You never have to save or send anything. Every answer goes to Stephen the moment you give it. Close the page whenever you like and come back to the same link; it will be exactly as you left it.
